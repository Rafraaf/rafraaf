About

Rafraaf Studios

رَفراف

Rafraaf is a family entertainment studio that makes films and series for every member of the family. We tell stories that carry the feeling of a homecoming dinner after time away and longing: whimsical, warm and full of heart, led by characters who look like us and worlds of imagination. We develop experiences rooted in our culture and in the small details an outsider wouldn't understand. And when the lights go down and we appear on your screens, we hope you feel safe and excited, and that everything will be okay.

Our stories begin in Saudi Arabia, but they are woven to reach Arab and Muslim families everywhere.

Why Rafraaf?

The rafraaf is a kingfisher that lives among the mangroves of the Red Sea coast. It hovers over the water, fluttering, then dives into the water to another world. That is what we hope every story does: catch your attention, then carry you to a new world where you forget your own.

Founder

Rafraaf was founded by Mariam Khayat, an animation producer and writer from Jeddah, whose films have won many local and international awards, from the Saudi Film Festival to Egypt, Ireland and the US. She has more than 15 years of experience in animation, film production and education. She holds a Master of Fine Arts from the University of Southern California.
