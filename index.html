About

Rafraaf Studios

رَفراف

Rafraaf is a Saudi family entertainment studio that creates original films and series for the whole family to enjoy together, and, in time, games.

We make stories that feel like coming home: whimsical, cozy and full of heart. Our characters are lovable, our worlds are full of wonder, and our stories are rooted in our language, our culture and the everyday moments families recognise. When the lights go down, we want every family to feel the same quiet promise: you're in good hands, and everything will be okay.

Our stories begin in Saudi Arabia, but they're made to travel to families across the Arab world and Muslim households everywhere.

Why Rafraaf?

The rafraaf is a kingfisher that lives in the mangroves of the Red Sea coast. It hovers above the water, fluttering in place, then dives through the surface into another world. That's what we hope every story does: it catches your eye, holds you for a moment, then carries you somewhere new. The name itself comes from the Arabic rafrafa, the sound of wings fluttering.

Founder

Rafraaf was founded by Mariam Khayat, an animation producer and director based in Jeddah, whose animated short The Menace from Above won at the Saudi Film Festival in 2023. She brings more than 15 years of experience across animation, film production and education.
